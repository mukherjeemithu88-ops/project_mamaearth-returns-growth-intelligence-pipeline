**Situation**
Mamaearth's regional operations and finance teams are reviewing baseline commercial performance following data reconciliation. Cleaned total revenue stands at INR 97,358.30, compared to a raw total revenue of INR 99,860.20, accounting for a duplicate reconciliation delta of INR 2,501.90. Performance tracking identifies the true peak month as March 2026, delivering verified revenue of INR 20,318.90. Meanwhile, January 2026 was identified as an outlier inflated month, where apparent revenue was initially recorded at INR 29,582.10 against a corrected revenue of INR 11,637.10.

**Complication**
Operational workflows and finance projections face severe pressure from elevated product returns concentrated in specific fulfillment channels. Across payment methods, Cash on Delivery (COD) exhibits a return rate of 44.4%, substantially higher than UPI at 18.9% and CARD at 14.7%. The highest risk segment occurs in Tier 2 cities using COD, where the return rate reaches 54.5%. For operations, more than half of all orders dispatched to this segment result in reverse logistics, while regional finance must account for heavy revenue erosion and payment-related variance driven by COD dependency.

**Resolution**
Regional leadership must align operational capacity and financial planning directly with these validated figures. Finance models must adopt cleaned revenue benchmarks—anchoring top-line expectations around the March 2026 performance of INR 20,318.90 and adjusting historical baseline models away from the outlier figures of January 2026. Simultaneously, operations and finance heads must prioritize intervention in Tier 2 COD orders to curb the 54.5% return rate, shifting promotional emphasis toward lower-risk channels such as CARD (14.7% return rate) and UPI (18.9% return rate) to stabilize fulfillment pipelines.
